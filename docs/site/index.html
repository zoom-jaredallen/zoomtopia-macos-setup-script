Set up the Mac

  1. Log into the account students will use. Connect power and the approved network.
  2. Download Zoomtopia-Setup.zip from that release, expand it, and open Zoomtopia Setup.app. Confirm the normal macOS first-launch dialog.
  3. Click Start Setup and review the full or limited run. The app checks current vendor installers and automatically skips current apps or upgrades older versions. After verification, enter administrator credentials in the macOS authorization dialog.
  4. Complete all four Zoom checks. Resolve any warnings or requested restart and rerun setup before marking the Mac ready.
  5. For macOS updates, use Authorize Update / Restart… to complete installation in Apple Software Update. Reopen setup after any restart and verify again.

If a download or launch is blocked

Contact the staging lead or corporate IT. They can approve the app and download destinations or provide the approved offline installers. Do not disable macOS security settings.
